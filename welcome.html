What Happens Next

Your first week

Your membership covers padel at Padel Logan and golf at Meadowbrook Golf Club, so there are two sides to get set up. We take care of both.

  1. We confirm your membership. Our team checks your application and confirms your Founding Membership by email, usually within two business days.
  2. Your golf membership is set up. Meadowbrook Golf Club sets up the golf membership included with your tier and will be in touch about your GolfLink and handicap if you need one.
  3. Your padel benefits switch on. We link your MATCHi account so non-peak court hire is free and peak drops to $10, and open up bookings two weeks ahead.
  4. Come and play. Book a court, join a social session at your level, or use the private lesson included in your membership to get started.

Anything at all, we are on 07 3299 6653 or info@padellogan.com.au. Keep your Stripe receipt — it is your tax invoice.
