How to Book

Booking runs through MATCHi

Padel Logan takes court bookings through MATCHi, the booking platform used by racket sports venues worldwide. Setting up an account is free and takes a minute.

  1. Create a free MATCHi account. You will need one to hold a booking, and Founding Members need it so their $100 MATCHi credit lands on the right account.
  2. Find Padel Logan. Search for us in MATCHi and you will see live court availability.
  3. Pick your court and time. Choose a slot, confirm, and you are on.
