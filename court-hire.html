Questions

Before you book

How much does it cost to hire a padel court in Logan?

Off peak, 6:00am to 4:00pm, a court is $60 an hour. Peak, 4:00pm to 10:00pm, a court is $90 an hour. All rates include GST. A court fits four players, so that works out at $15 each off peak and $22.50 each at peak.

Do I need to be a member to book a court?

No. Everyone is welcome to book a court, member or not. You can book and pay online, or call the club on 07 3299 6653 and we will do it for you.

How many people do you need to play padel?

Padel is played in doubles, two against two, so a court fits four players and the cost splits four ways. You can book with two if you prefer. The court rate is the same either way.

What time can I play?

The courts are open 6:00am to 10:00pm, Monday to Sunday. Off peak runs 6:00am to 4:00pm and peak runs 4:00pm to 10:00pm.

Do I need my own racquet?

No. Racquets and balls are available at the club. If you want to check before you come, call 07 3299 6653.

Where are the courts?

Padel Logan is inside Meadowbrook Golf Club at 2 to 22 Golf Course Drive, Meadowbrook, about 20 minutes south of Brisbane and 30 minutes north of the Gold Coast. Park at the clubhouse.

Is it cheaper with a membership?

Yes, if you play regularly. Bronze is $125 a month, which makes off peak court hire free and peak $10 each. Founding Memberships run twelve months and add unlimited golf at Meadowbrook Golf Club.
