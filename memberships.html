Membership Terms and Conditions
  • All Founding Memberships are valid for an initial 12 month membership term from the commencement date.
  • Founding Membership pricing, structure and inclusions apply to the initial 12 month membership term only.
  • Renewal pricing, membership structure and inclusions for Year 2 and beyond are subject to change at the discretion of Padel Logan.
  • Any changes to renewal pricing or membership inclusions will be communicated prior to the expiry of the initial 12 month membership term.
