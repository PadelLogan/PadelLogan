Team building on court

Padel is the rare team activity where nobody sits out. It is played four up, the rallies start within minutes of picking up a racquet, and a complete beginner can hold their own against a regular. Add the golf course, driving range and mini golf at Meadowbrook and you have a full day in one place, 20 minutes from Brisbane CBD.

Team Building

Everyone plays.
That is the point.

Most corporate sport days split a group into the people who can play and the people watching. Padel does not work like that.

01
Learned in minutes

The court is enclosed and the racquets are solid, so the ball stays in play. Groups are rallying almost straight away instead of chasing balls around a court.

02
Mixed ability, same game

Played four up in doubles, padel levels the field. Fitness and experience matter far less than they do in tennis, so teams mix across ages and abilities.

03
Short, social formats

Rotations, round robins and mini tournaments keep everyone moving between courts and partners, so the group actually mixes rather than staying in its usual huddles.

Build the day around your group

Every group is a different size with a different amount of time, so we put packages together to suit rather than selling a fixed menu. Tell us the numbers and the window and we will come back with options and pricing.

Full Day
Full Day

The whole venue for the day. Padel on the courts with a coach running the session, plus the golf course, driving range or mini golf, and food and drink through the Meadowbrook clubhouse. Suits conference days, end of year functions and client events.

Half Day
Half Day

A shorter format built around the courts. A coached padel session run as a round robin or mini tournament, with the option to add the driving range or mini golf and finish with food and drink.

Social
Social Padel

Courts booked for your group to run yourselves. No coach, no structure, just the courts and equipment for a team to turn up and play. The simplest and most affordable way to get a group on court.

These are a starting point rather than a fixed menu. Tell us your group size, the time you have and the kind of day you want, and we will put something together to suit.
